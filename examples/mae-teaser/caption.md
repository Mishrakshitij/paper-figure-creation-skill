**Masked autoencoding concept and training trade-off.** The schematic selects four of sixteen illustrative patches; the encoder processes visible patches and the decoder reconstructs missing content. The plot redraws the three ViT-L rows of Table 2 in He et al., *Masked Autoencoders Are Scalable Vision Learners*. With an 8-block decoder, skipping mask tokens in the encoder changes pretraining time from 42.4 to 15.4 hours and fine-tuned top-1 accuracy from 84.2% to 84.9%. The 1-block variant is also shown. All use 800 epochs, 75% masking, and 128 TPU-v3 cores. No uncertainty is supplied for these table entries. These are ablations, not an all-method leaderboard. [Source: Table 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2111.06377v3).
