**Transformer macro architecture.** Encoded source representations provide keys and values to decoder cross-attention, while the masked target path supplies queries. Embedding and prediction operations are outside the repeated blocks. Each attention/feed-forward sublayer uses the original post-norm residual structure, shown in the subtitle and omitted from the wiring for clarity; dropout and autoregressive token feedback are also omitted. The arrows leaving stack-level blocks denote the final encoder or decoder output. Original schematic based on [Vaswani et al., Figure 1 and Section 3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1706.03762).

Alt text: A source encoder conditions a target decoder through cross-attention; masking restricts target self-attention and the output head predicts the next token.
